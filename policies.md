# Bingo & Gogo Pizza - Store Policies & FAQs

## Ordering & Cancellation Policies
* **Order Modifications:** Changes or additions to an order must be called in within 5 minutes of order placement. Once pizza dough has entered the oven, toppings or sizes cannot be altered.
* **Cancellations:** 
  * Takeout/Delivery: Cancellations are accepted for a full refund within 10 minutes of placing the order.
  * Catering/Large Orders ($100+): Cancellations require at least 2 hours' advance notice for a full refund.

---

## Refund & Issue Resolution Policy
* **Missing Items or Incorrect Orders:** Contact us immediately at (555) 839-7499. We will send a driver with the correct item free of charge or issue a store credit.
* **Quality Concerns:** If you are unsatisfied with the quality of your food, return at least 50% of the untouched product within 2 hours of receipt for a replacement or full refund.

---

## Catering & Large Group Orders
* **Advance Notice:** Orders for more than 8 large pizzas require at least 24 hours' advance notice.
* **Catering Discount:** 10% off on all orders over $200 (applied automatically at checkout).
* **Tax Exemption:** Tax-exempt organizations must present a tax exemption certificate at the time of payment.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

**Q: Do you offer Halal options?**
A: All chicken toppings (grilled chicken and wings) are certified Halal. Our beef and pork items are processed separately.

**Q: Can I half-and-half toppings on a pizza?**
A: Yes, half-and-half toppings are allowed on Medium and Large specialty or build-your-own pizzas. The price is charged based on the higher-priced half.

**Q: Do you have a loyalty program?**
A: Yes! Our "Bingo Rewards" program gives you 1 point for every $1 spent. 100 points = $10 off your next order. Sign up via our website or in-store terminal.

**Q: Are dogs allowed in the restaurant?**
A: Service animals are permitted indoors. Non-service pets are welcome in our outdoor patio seating area only.
