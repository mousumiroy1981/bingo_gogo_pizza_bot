## Service Modes

### 1. Dine-In
* Indoor seating capacity for up to 35 guests.
* First-come, first-served basis; no reservations required.

### 2. Takeout / Curbside Pickup
* Orders can be placed online, by phone, or in person.
* Average preparation time: 15–20 minutes.
* Designated curbside pickup spots marked at the front of the store.

### 3. Delivery
* **Delivery Radius:** Up to 5 miles from our store address.
* **Minimum Order for Delivery:** $15.00 (before taxes and delivery fee).
* **Delivery Fee:** $3.99 standard flat fee within 3 miles; $5.99 for 3 to 5 miles.
* **Average Delivery Time:** 35–50 minutes depending on peak traffic.
